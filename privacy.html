Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Gento runs on your Mac. Your presentations, text, images and settings stay on your Mac and are never sent to us.

What Gento sends over the internet

  • Licence check (after the beta): your licence key and a name for your Mac (for example "Ahmad's MacBook Pro") go to Dodo Payments' licence service when you activate, about once a week to confirm the key is still valid, and when you deactivate. This lets one key work on 2 Macs.
  • Update check: once a day Gento downloads a small list of versions from GitHub to see whether a new version is available, and downloads the update if there is one.

Gento has no analytics, tracking or advertising.

Purchases

When you buy Gento, Dodo Payments (our Merchant of Record) collects your name, email and payment details to process the order and send your key. See Dodo Payments' privacy policy. We receive your name, email and order details from them for support and refunds.

Feedback

If you contact us on Telegram, we see what you send there and use it only to answer you and improve Gento.
