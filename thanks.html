Thank you for buying Gento!

Your licence key is on your receipt and in an email from Dodo Payments (our reseller). It works on 2 Macs, for good.

  1. Already have Gento? Skip to step 3. If not, download it:
    Download Gento for Mac
  2. Install it: open the download, drag Gento to Applications and open it. The first time, macOS asks you to allow it: open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Activate: click the Gento icon in the menu bar, choose Licence…, paste your key and click Activate.

Good to know

Updates are free

Gento updates itself when PowerPoint is closed. Nothing to download again.

New Mac?

On the old Mac open Licence… and click Deactivate This Mac, then enter the key on the new one.

No email?

Check spam for a message from Dodo Payments. Still nothing? Message Geno on Telegram with the email you paid with.
