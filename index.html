What's in the tab

Guides that think

Spacing, grids, repeat

Even gaps, perfect grids and copies in rows, with a preview of the result before you commit. Corner radius per corner, exactly.

Text styles, finally

Save a look once, apply it anywhere. Follows your theme fonts, Arabic included. Export your styles and share them.

Line, paragraph and letter spacing that follow your mouse with no lag (about 9 ms a step). Cancel puts everything back.

A timeline you can drag, ready-made presets, Animate To, motion-path tools, and your own presets saved by kind. Add, add first, or replace.

Illustrator to slides

Pick an .ai file and get one slide per artboard, with native shapes, real gradients and editable text. No Illustrator needed.
