Power tools for PowerPoint on Mac

Gento adds the tools Mac PowerPoint is missing: a toolbar that docks under the ribbon, an animation timeline you can drag, SVG paste as editable shapes, your own icon library, deck cleaning and more. Every tool lives in a new Gento tab.

What Gento does

Docked toolbar

Your favourite commands in one row under the ribbon, each with a keyboard shortcut (⌃1 to ⌃0). Search any command with ⌃⇧P.

Animation timeline

See every animation on the slide as bars. Drag to change delay and duration, reorder, stagger, play.

Paste SVG

Copy SVG code or Illustrator art and paste it as editable PowerPoint shapes in one click.

Library

Point Gento at your icon and image folders. Browse with live previews; click to insert.

Corner radius

Set exact rounded corners in px, pt or cm, and make every corner in the deck match.

Arrange

Spacing up and down, stack shapes, mirror positions around the slide's centre.

Text tools

Fit, wrap, swap the text of two shapes, turn text into vector shapes, set the proofing language deck-wide.

Review

Sticky notes, Draft/Confidential stamps, progress bars, a slide summary and mini-slides.

Cleaner

Finds stray fonts, double spaces, empty placeholders, off-slide objects and unused layouts, and fixes them after you approve.

Colour

A floating colour bar with your theme and brand palettes, and deck-wide colour replace.

Agenda & breadcrumbs

Agenda slides, section dividers and breadcrumbs built from your sections, kept in sync.

Smart tools

Fields you change once and update everywhere, shape carousels, and Chart+ to format many charts at once.
